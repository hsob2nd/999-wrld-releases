# 999 WRLD

A Juice WRLD inspired music player for your computer and phone.

- Local music library, albums, search and alternate song titles
- 31-band EQ and automatic volume matching
- Local radio with an optional AI DJ, natural male/female voices and adjustable energy
- Juice WRLD catalogue, live radio and song-version comparisons
- Normal, smart and Loved songs playlists
- Original-quality offline playlist downloads
- Lyrics, tag editing and a non-destructive audio editor
- Listen counts and monthly recaps
- Custom colours, backgrounds and appearance
- Installable iPhone web app
